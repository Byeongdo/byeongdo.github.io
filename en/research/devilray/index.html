Devilray: A Reconfigurable Adversarial Baseline for Revealing Blind Spots in Fake Base Station Detection

Finding the blind spots in fake base station detection

Taekkyung Oh, Duckwoo Kim, Hansung Bae, Beomseok Oh, CheolJun Park, Tyler Tucker, Nathaniel Bennett, Sangwook Bae, Byeongdo Hong, Patrick Traynor, Yongdae Kim
Fake base stationsDetectionLTE

Overview

A configurable baseline for evaluating fake base station detectors. Grounded in observed commercial behavior and cellular specifications, it explores how different adversarial behaviors expose gaps in detector assumptions.

Why it matters

Systematic evaluation across varied adversarial behaviors helps researchers test the limits of detection and develop defenses that generalize beyond a small set of known attacks.
