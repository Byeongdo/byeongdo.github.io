GUTI Reallocation Demystified: Cellular Location Tracking with Changing Temporary Identifier

When changing an identifier is not enough

Byeongdo Hong, Sangwook Bae, Yongdae Kim

Byeongdo Hong · First author

LTEIdentityLocation privacy

Overview

Investigates how predictable LTE temporary-identifier reassignment can undermine location privacy even when identifiers change, based on measurements of operational cellular networks.

Why it matters

Privacy depends on how identifiers change, not only on whether they change. The work examines the gap between a privacy mechanism and its deployment in real networks.
