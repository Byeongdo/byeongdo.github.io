Passive Multi-Target GUTI Identification via Visual-RF Correlation in LTE Networks

Privacy at the intersection of visual and radio signals

Byeongdo Hong, Gunwoo Yoon

Byeongdo Hong · First author · Corresponding author

LTEIdentityLocation privacy

Overview

Studies how the timing of visible device use and radio control-plane events can associate LTE temporary identifiers with multiple devices through passive observation, and examines the location-privacy implications of persistent identifiers.

Why it matters

Highlights privacy risks that emerge when observations from different modalities are combined, motivating closer attention to temporary-identifier lifetimes and reassignment policies.
