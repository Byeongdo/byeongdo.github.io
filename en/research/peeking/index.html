IEEE Transactions on Mobile Computing · 17(10), 2366–2380

Peeking Over the Cellular Walled Gardens — A Method for Closed Network Diagnosis

Finding performance improvements through protocol diagnosis

Byeongdo Hong, Shinjo Park, Hongil Kim, Dongkwan Kim, Hyunwook Hong, Hyunwoo Choi, Jean-Pierre Seifert, Sung-Ju Lee, Yongdae Kim

Byeongdo Hong · First author

Protocol diagnosisPerformanceControl plane

Overview

Proposes a method for diagnosing cellular-network performance problems by comparing control-plane behavior across operators, with a case study of circuit-switched fallback.

Why it matters

Protocol messages offer a way to investigate performance problems even when internal network operations are not directly accessible. Comparing deployed network behavior helps identify where improvements may be needed.
